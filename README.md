# Year 12–13 IB Diploma Psychology (SL & HL — NEW FA2027) Study Guide

Interactive study guide, procedural drill engine, mock-exam suite, 6-Concepts & Research Methods Toolkit, and Research Proposal IA workspace for **IB Diploma Psychology (NEW First Assessment May 2027)** — covering both DP1 and DP2 with a live **SL / HL toggle**.

## Highlights
- **100% Aligned to the NEW FA2027 Guide:** Built around the **6 Core Concepts** (*Bias, Causality, Change, Measurement, Perspective, Responsibility*), **3 Core Approaches** (*Biological, Cognitive, Sociocultural*), **4 Compulsory Contexts** (*Health & Well-being, Human Development, Human Relationships, Learning & Cognition*), **4 Class Practicals**, **HL Extensions** (*Culture, Motivation, Technology*), and **HL Paper 3** (30%).
- **FA2027 Mock Papers (`/exam`):** Paper 1 (SAQs, unseen contexts & concept ERQs), Paper 2 (class practicals & unseen-study concept evaluation), HL Paper 3 (quantitative & qualitative data analysis), plus a 25-Q Diagnostic MCQ Bank.
- **FA2027 Research Proposal IA (`/ia`):** Complete 4×6 criteria guide (Introduction, Research Methodology, Data Collection Tool, Discussion), 2,200-word budget, *"NO data collection"* guardrails, and interactive self-audit.
- **Zero-Login iPad Persistence:** All progress, Daily 5, SRS review, streaks, and SL/HL selection save automatically in `localStorage`.
